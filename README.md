## what i work on

### ai

- **local serving & inference** - running and evaluating models locally.
- **applied ml & dl** - classification, recommendations, and applied models.
- **federated learning** - experimenting with capability isolation and access to shared models.
- **dllms** - exploring diffusion language models and comparing them with autoregressive models.
- **model behavior & evaluation** - reproducible experiments on bias, reliability, and failure modes.

### agents

- **agentic workflows** - tool use, multi-step execution, and coding agents.
- **mcp, plugins & skills** - connecting agents to tools and extending their behavior.
- **tool selection & trust** - studying how agents choose between legitimate, malicious, and decoy tools.
- **privacy policy & information flow** - tracking sensitive data across tools, memory, and agent actions.
- **prompt injection** - measuring attacks and defenses across languages and tool surfaces.

### dev tools

- **cli & tui** - terminal-first tools and interfaces.
- **git & contribution tooling** - version control, review workflows, and open-source contribution tools.
- **agent infrastructure** - building, extending, and evaluating coding agents.

### low level

- **linux installation & recovery** - boot flows, dual boot, rollback, and hardware support.
- **test automation** - vm-based installation scenarios and system validation.
- **custom os** - exploring practical desktop systems and lower-level software.
- **native & cross-platform software** - building tools that run across windows, macos, and linux.
- **fuzzing** - experimenting with harness generation and execution feedback, including dllm-guided approaches.

### etc

- **web apps & services** - applications, apis, bots, and community tools.
- **games & simulations** - playable systems and interactive experiments.
- **terminal graphics** - rendering games, video, and 3d scenes in a terminal.
